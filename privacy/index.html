Privacy Policy

Resonance · Last updated 17 October 2026

Resonance is a private app for two people in a relationship. Everything you add is shared only with your partner. We don't show ads, we don't sell or rent your data, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

Who we are

Resonance is run by Global Tether Ventures Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 16765434), registered office 45 Silverstream House, Fitzroy Street, London W1T 6EB. We're responsible for your data (the "controller" under UK data protection law). You can reach us at contact@globaltetherventures.com.

What we store

  • Account: your email address and password (stored securely as a hash), your display name, your city and your time zone.
  • What you share with your partner: photos for the daily game and "Your world", themes, mosaics, question answers, words, ratings and reactions, moods, trips, important dates, the shared background, "thinking of you" hearts, your lamp, postcards (the photo, your message, the place and its stamp), open-when letters (written, or recorded as a voice note), your firsts, Someday lists and photos, and Our list.
  • Talking points: notes of things to bring up next time you talk. These are private to you unless you choose to share one with your partner.
  • Your postal address (optional): if you add one so your partner can send you printed postcards. Only you can see it; your partner is only told whether one has been added. We use it only to post cards to you, and you can remove it at any time.
  • Payments: if you pay for Resonance+, your card is handled by Stripe and we never see your full card details. We keep your plan, its status and renewal date, your Stripe customer reference and your billing country (so we know where we can sell).
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, a push address for your phone so we can tell you about your partner's activity and send reminders.

Calls: the window, holding hands and the sofa

When you open the window or sit on the sofa, your voice (and, on the sofa, your camera if you turn it on) goes straight between your device and your partner's, encrypted. If your networks can't connect directly, it passes through a relay run by Cloudflare, still encrypted; the relay can't listen in and keeps nothing. We never record calls. Messages and reactions on the sofa are only passed between the two of you while you're there; we don't keep them.

Virtual backgrounds on the sofa are worked out on your own device. If you upload a picture to use, it never leaves your device: only your video, with the picture behind you, goes to your partner. The tool that finds you in the picture is downloaded from Google's free MediaPipe library; it runs on your device and sends nothing back.

Where you are

Resonance never asks for your location. It uses the city and time zone you choose. For "Their sky", we look up the weather for that city's name with Open-Meteo, which never receives anything about you.

Why we store it

To run Resonance for you, as you've asked us to (our contract with you): to sign you in, pair you with your partner, show each of you what the other has shared, connect your calls, take payment for Resonance+, post printed postcards and send the notifications you've turned on. We also use crash reports to keep the app working (our legitimate interest in fixing problems). We don't use your data for advertising or profiling.

Who can see it

Only you and the partner you pair with. Content is protected by access rules so no other account can read it.

We use a small number of service providers to run Resonance:

  • Supabase hosts the database, sign-in and the photos and voice notes you share.
  • Stripe takes payments for Resonance+ on the website.
  • Cloudflare relays calls when your devices can't connect directly.
  • Expo, together with Apple and Google push services, delivers notifications. These receive only the notification text and your phone's push address.
  • Sentry receives a report if the app crashes: the error, the screen it happened on and the device model. Reports don't include your name, email, photos or anything you wrote.
  • Open-Meteo provides the weather for your city's name.
  • Our print partner, once printed postcards are available, receives the postcard and the recipient's name and address, only to print and post it.

Some of these providers process data outside the UK. When they do, they use the safeguards UK law requires, such as the UK's international data transfer agreements. We don't share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

On the website

The web app keeps you signed in and remembers small settings using your browser's own storage. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, we remove your account, your photos, answers, moods, address and everything else that's only yours. Things you made together (such as trips and important dates) stay with your partner until they delete their account too. If you're the last one left, the shared space is deleted as well. We keep records of payments for as long as the law requires for accounting, usually six years.

Unpairing

Either of you can end the pairing from the app (menu, then your partner, then Unpair). This deletes your shared space for both of you: the photos, mosaics, answers, words, letters, postcards, trips, dates and background you shared. Your accounts stay, and each of you can pair again.

Deleting your account

In the app, open the menu, then Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and data straight away. You can also email us at contact@globaltetherventures.com and we'll delete it for you.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how we use it, at any time by emailing contact@globaltetherventures.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Age

You need to be 16 or over to use Resonance, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and stored with a provider that encrypts it at rest. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep access limited to what the app needs.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top, and tell you in the app about anything important.
